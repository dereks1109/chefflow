# Unit Conversion Tables

Complete reference for weight, volume, and temperature conversions.

## Weight Conversions

| From | To | Factor |
|------|-----|--------|
| g | kg | ÷ 1000 |
| kg | g | × 1000 |
| g | oz | ÷ 28.35 |
| oz | g | × 28.35 |
| oz | lb | ÷ 16 |
| lb | oz | × 16 |
| lb | g | × 453.6 |
| g | lb | ÷ 453.6 |

**Quick Facts:**
- 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb
- 1 oz ≈ 28 g
- 1 lb ≈ 454 g

| °C | °F |
|----|-----|
| 0 | 32 |
| 20 | 68 |
| 37 | 98.6 |
| 100 | 212 |

**Common Cooking Temperatures:**

| Purpose | °C | °F |
|---------|----|----|
| Refrigerate | 4 | 39 |
| Room temp | 20 | 68 |
| Simmer | 82 | 180 |
| Gentle boil | 95 | 203 |

- °F to °C: `(°F - 32) × 5/9`

## Common Unit Mistakes

❌ **fl oz vs oz**
- fl oz = fluid ounce (volume, 30 ml)
- oz = ounce (weight, 28 g)
- NOT interchangeable!

❌ **Cup variations**
- US cup = 237 ml
- Metric cup = 250 ml
- Australian cup = 250 ml

❌ **Spoon confusion**
- tsp = teaspoon = 5 ml
- tbsp = tablespoon = 15 ml
- Different by 3x!

---

## When to Use Density

**Use for:**
- Baking (flour, sugar, butter)
- General cooking (oil, honey)

**Don't use for:**
- Meat/protein (measure by weight)
- Liquids without fat (assume 1g/ml)
- Variable items (leafy greens, nuts)

## Rounding Examples

| Calculated | Rounded | Reason |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| 1237 g | 1200-1250 g | Large amount → nearest 0.5 |
| 47 ml | 47 ml | Medium → keep as-is |
| 2.8 tsp | 2.75 or 3 tsp | Small → nearest 0.25 or whole |
| 0.33 cup | 1/3 cup | Use fractions for clarity |

---

## Notes

- Conversions are approximate (rounding for cooking)
- Weight is more precise than volume
- Density varies by ingredient (flour packs differently)
- For baking, use weight when possible
- Cooking is forgiving; volume works fine
